# Front Page Foundry audit — economy

*Evidence paths in this report (`scratch/…`, `~/.cache/fpf-audit/…`) pointed into the auditor's private copy and were not kept; the regression tests named here are in `tests/Sim.Tests/Audit*Tests.cs` and `game/Main.SelfTest.cs`. Merge notes: `docs/AUDIT-2026-09-23.md`.*

Scope: `src/Sim/Market.cs`, `Difficulty.cs`, `Paper.cs`, `Prestige.cs`, `Town.cs`, `MapGen.cs`, `Noise.cs`, `Random.cs`,
and the money / credit / interest / default / warning / share / notices / calendar / land / town code in `World.cs`.
Regression tests: `tests/Sim.Tests/AuditEconomyTests.cs` (21) and `AuditViabilityTests.cs` (2). Run against the untouched RC
`src/` (stashed), 22 of the 23 fail; the exception is `TheTownGrowsTheSameAfterASaveAndLoad`, whose bug (E13) is masked on the RC by E5
(the town never grows). `AuditViabilityTests` is a measurement with sanity bounds, not a regression test.

## Coordination notes

- **Docks and the production auditor's P11** (`Dock.cs` `Affordable`, keeps next week's interest before buying on credit): E1 already covers docks, because `Dock.Update` asks `world.CanAfford`, which now keeps back two weekly payments. The two overlap; merged together a dock just keeps back a little more. E1 is still needed for every other purchase path (ads, plans, redo, vehicles, signals).
- **X4 (resale perf)** is E20: cached per building type and recomputed only when that type's count changes, so it needs no invalidation at the `owned[]` write sites (including `Save.cs` `ReadState`).
- **Arbitrage**: the production auditor's independent proof (`production/scratch/ScratchProofs.cs.txt`, +$1,599.36 in 7 days) is the same bug as E3; the fix is in `Market.cs` only.

## Proposals (not implemented)

- **P1 (§16 change): weekly profit = operating result, not net-worth change.** Today every purchase is booked as a loss of 25% of its price plus land, so an expanding company "loses money" every week it invests and 4× that dominates the share price (clamped at $0 now, E2). Suggest profit = sales − dock purchases − interest (land/depreciation could be spread), which makes the share price and town growth reward trade, not idleness.
- **P2 (gamelayer): say why a purchase is refused near the limit.** With E1 the bank keeps back two weeks' interest (Steady: 2% of the balance), so an ad can read red with a little credit showing. A clipping/cursor note ("the bank keeps back two weeks' interest") would explain it; `CanAfford` already drives the red price, so ads and placement agree.
- **P3 (gamelayer copy, optional):** copper strike / oil gusher / cotton tumble now cheapen the good at depots too; "cheap at the docks" is still true but could read "cheap everywhere this week".
- **P4 (map, new companies only):** §16 says no hills within 24×16 of the works, but 64 of 200 seeds have hill tiles there (up to 274; +25% land for machines, rail 3×), and forest is kept out only of 20×14. Harmless in play; changing it alters terrain under existing saves, so only worth doing behind a map version.
- **P5 (balance): town size now that it grows.** At §16's rate (1 + trailing profit/$500 blocks every 3 days, max 4) a normal profitable works hits the cap by week 4, so ~2,000 blocks (~18k tiles, radius ~75 around the river crossing, covering the start area's free ground) by the 50-hour mark; a growth step costs ~5 ms at that size (measured 2.2 ms at 1k blocks, 5 ms at 2k, 10 ms at 6k). If that is too much town, `GrowEveryDays` or the $500 step are the knobs.
- **P6 (balance): the tutorial's press.** Ed. 4 sends the player to a $3,000 press that repays in ~9.5 weeks on the one-smelter works it teaches; a second smelter ($636) roughly doubles income first.
- **P7 (gamelayer polish, unreachable in practice):** commission names past round 11 fall back to "{n}th" ("21th", "Zeppelin 10th" from round 9).

## Suspected / out of scope (not fixed)

- **S1 (haulage):** a self-built Motor Truck delivered to a truck depot is enlisted free and can then be scrapped for 75% of $3,600 = $2,700 regardless of the Motor Truck market (K = 30 halves the depot price after ~30 sales): a glut-free sale route for mass truck production. `World.Enlisted` / `ExecuteScrap`. Not tested.
- **S2 (town):** the town may grow under a trestle's deck (the spanned cells are not buildings); harmless until the player wants that ground back.
- **S3:** commission quantity/goodwill arithmetic overflows past commission ~375 (1.5^62); unreachable.
- GDD §10's event table still lists "Default … Grace counter +1" (superseded by §16: one missed payment ends it) and buy-only supply events (E3). Doc update for the lead.
