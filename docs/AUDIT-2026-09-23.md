# Audit — 2026-09-23 (0.9.0-rc1 → 0.9.0-rc2)

Nolan asked: *"audit the game and fix any bugs and make improvements where needed."* Five auditors each took
one area in an isolated copy of the release candidate. The lead then checked every fix, merged the five sets, and
reconciled where two auditors had fixed the same thing. The full reports, with evidence, are in `docs/audit/`.

**Result:** 91 fixes: logistics 9, production 13, economy 20, haulage and saves 14, game layer 33, lead 2. Six
were found by two auditors and are counted twice, so there are about 85 distinct bugs. About 18 proposals and
decisions are left for Nolan (below). Each sim fix has a test that fails on 0.9.0-rc1 (checked per patch: 13, 12, 21
and 21 of the new logistics, production, economy and haulage tests fail there; the rest are guards and measurements).
The game-layer fixes are covered by 53 new self-test checks (10 of them fail on rc1) and before/after screenshots.

| | 0.9.0-rc1 | 0.9.0-rc2 |
|---|---|---|
| Sim tests (`dotnet test tests/Sim.Tests`) | 127 | **217**, all pass |
| Self-test checks (`-- --selftest`) | 132 | **185**, pass in the editor, the exported Linux build, and the Windows build under Proton 11 |

## What Nolan will notice

- **Saves are safe.** Founding a company can no longer write over another company's folder. The founding page offers a free name ("Carvell Falls Works 2") and refuses taken or empty names. A damaged or unreadable save no longer stops the game from starting.
- **Bankruptcy is always warned.** Purchases keep the next two weekly interest payments in hand. The Banker's Warning is checked every in-game hour. Each week is charged at the rate posted when the week began. A fold therefore comes at least a week after a warning. After the fold, Esc still opens the front office and Tab still opens the archive.
- **The paper explains dead works.** FIRES OUT AT THE WORKS prints when the starter coal runs out. The F overlay shows cold machines and mines in red. A machine blocked by coal it will not take says so. Tutorial edition 2 now tells the player to give coal its own belt.
- **The tutorial always finishes.** A player who never buys the $3,000 press no longer stops at edition 4. The telegram desk now always opens.
- **Tutorial money figures match the preset.** Editions 1 and 5 quote the preset's own cash and credit instead of Steady Trade's.
- **The town grows,** as §16 designs it. In 0.9.0-rc1 it stopped after 1–3 blocks.
- **Market prices behave as designed:**
  - Trends pull back toward normal at the designed rate.
  - Supply events move depot prices as well as dock prices, so a dock belted into a depot can no longer make money.
  - Every event makes the paper, not just the first of each kind.
- **Seam richness follows the preset.** Boom Times seams are 150% as rich and Hard Times 70%. On load, a Boom company's seam percentages rise.
- **Trains and vehicles:**
  - Two trains on one signalled line no longer deadlock.
  - Vehicles heading home are drawn facing and moving the right way.
  - Barges can follow the whole river.
- **Undo is exact and cannot make money.** Building, demolishing, pencilling and undoing in any order is money-neutral. Undo also puts back a building's filter, order and coal.
- **Faster:**
  - Power grids rebuild in 4 ms instead of 318 ms on a large electric works.
  - The daily autosave compresses off the main thread, removing a ~130 ms hitch at 42k structures.
  - A cached resale value halves the tick time with many docks.
- **The masthead and the controls hint read right:**
  - The hint shows every control.
  - A negative figure prints as "-$1.44".
  - The share price floors at $0.
  - Depot clippings name what the depot actually sells.

## Needs Nolan (decisions, one line each)

1. **Town size.** At §16's rate (up to 4 blocks every 3 days) a profitable works has about 2,000 blocks by hour 50. Turn it down with `Town.GrowEveryDays` or the $500 step if that is too much.
2. **Train entry rule (refines §16).** A train enters a block only while no other train holds that block or any block further along its way. This is what ends the deadlock. Trains waiting at stations are the visible effect.
3. **Coal mixed into ore belts.** It still jams a machine, as in the genre. rc2 makes the jam visible and teaches around it. Changing the rule (for example, a starved machine always takes coal) is your call.
4. **Share price definition.** Weekly profit is the change in net worth, so every purchase counts as a loss. Proposal: profit = sales − dock purchases − interest.
5. **Seasons.** GDD §11 promises snow on roofs, bare trees, autumn hatching and a seasonal masthead. None of it is built. It is a visual change, so it needs your approval.
6. **HUD above 1080p.** The HUD is fixed pixels and small at 2560×1440. Proposal: `stretch/mode=canvas_items` or a text-size setting.
7. **Home-made Motor Trucks** scrap for a flat $2,700 whatever the market pays: a glut-free sale route. Proposal: scrap them at market price.
8. **Hydro Dam cement delivery** (GDD §5 proposal) was never built. Build it, or strike it from §5.
9. **Newer-version saves.** A save from a newer build is treated as damaged and falls back to a backup. Proposal: at the next format bump, refuse the folder and say so.

Lead (merge) fixes on top:
- Each week's interest is charged at the rate posted when the week began. The merged self-test caught a BANK RATE RAISED event on payment day folding a company unwarned.
- A dam's lake now supersedes the plans it drowns; they used to stand as ghosts for good.
- Demolish + undo keeps the building's coal instead of refilling a 30-lump bag.
- The autosave compresses and writes on a worker thread; a failed save flashes in the masthead.
- Seam richness is applied once (two auditors had each applied it, which would have given ×2.25 on Boom).
- Docks use the shared purchase reserve.
- `Menu.SetName` renamed so it no longer hides Godot's `Node.SetName`.

## How it was checked

- Each auditor worked in an isolated copy. Godot ran in a private headless KWin with no sound, never touching the live desktop, speakers or save folder.
- Every patch's new tests were run on 0.9.0-rc1 to confirm they fail there. The lead merged the patches in order and re-ran everything after each merge.
- A copy of the live Boom Times company loads and plays on under rc2. It was taken read-only twice: day 7, and day 90 (revenue flat since day 11: no coal, tutorial stuck at edition 4).
- Exports: `godot --headless --export-release` for Linux and Windows. The self-test passes through the exported Linux binary and through the Windows `.exe` under Proton 11 (`steamuser` AppData log). This is the first run of the Windows build anywhere.
